Sample points about Twitter:

- Twitter is a social media platform where users share short messages called tweets.
- Tweets can contain text, images, videos, links, and hashtags.
- A hashtag helps connect posts about the same topic, such as #Tech or #News.
- Users can mention others using @username to start conversations or reply to posts.
- Retweets let people share someone else's tweet with their own followers.
- Likes and replies show engagement and interaction on the platform.
- Twitter is often used for real-time updates, breaking news, and public discussions.
- Many businesses use Twitter for branding, customer support, and marketing campaigns.
- Trending topics highlight popular subjects being discussed by many users.
- Twitter has influenced online communication, journalism, and social movements worldwide.
