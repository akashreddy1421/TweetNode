- Twitter, now known as X, is a social media platform for sharing short posts and updates.
- Users can follow accounts to see their posts in a personalized timeline.
- Posts can include text, photos, videos, links, and polls.
- Users can like, reply to, repost, and share posts.
- Hashtags help organize posts around topics and events.
- Search and trending topics help users discover current conversations.
- Direct messages let users communicate privately.
- People use Twitter to share news, opinions, entertainment, and updates from organizations and public figures.
